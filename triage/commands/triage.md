# Triage one item

Decide what should happen next to this item, for the people who maintain this project.

The item:

$INPUTS.item

You are in a checkout of the project. Read what you need: the code, the docs, the project's direction, its history. Change nothing.

End with exactly one outcome.

- **route**: the work should be done. Say whether it needs investigating, needs a plan, or can be delivered directly, and how big the smallest change that meets it would be.
- **asks**: a decision that belongs to a maintainer stands in the way, such as whether this should exist, whether it is this project's job, or which of two directions to take. Leave each as an ask: the context, the options with what each one costs, and the one you recommend.
- **close**: nothing should be built. It is already delivered, a duplicate, already possible with something that exists, not this project's job, or not actionable. Cite what makes it so: a file, a doc, a commit, another item.

Write the summary for a maintainer who has not read the item.

Fill the fields your outcome does not use with `none` or an empty list. Size is the changed lines of the smallest change: xs under 300, s under 600, m under 1000, l under 1700, xl above.
