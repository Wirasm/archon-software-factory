# Land policy: the pack's default

A project that names no policy of its own has allowed nothing to land without an operator: the land gate asks every time, and the change lands when an operator chooses `land`.

A change is merged as one squashed commit unless the project's policy says otherwise. After it landed, nothing is waited for.

A project's own land policy names which ready changes may land without an ask, how a change is merged, and what must hold on the branch after a change lands and what to do when it does not.
