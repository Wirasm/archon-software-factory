# Pinning a jelly on a phone removes its visible route back

Filed by Wirasm.

<!-- archon-discovery phone-pinned-jellies-unreachable -->
## Problem

At widths up to 900px, pinning a jelly from the stream removes it from view without a visible way to reopen or unpin it. A jelly without pending attention also has no route through the attention dock. Reloading preserves the inaccessible pinned state.

## Evidence

Verified by reading base `ac81d0303bdf7d2520ce06b4a2b9809cd4909c2b`:

- `assets/surface.html:102` hides the pinned panel at widths up to 900px.
- `assets/surface.html:280-284` offers Pin in stream entries.
- `assets/surface.html:305-307` excludes pinned jellies and removes their stream entries.
- `assets/surface.html:489-516` renders pinned jellies into the hidden panel.
- `assets/surface.html:602-613` connects Pin to the existing persistence endpoint; `assets/surface.html:615-624` provides reopening through pinned tiles and unpinning through the full-view header.
- The `p` shortcut at `assets/surface.html:643` toggles `nopinned`, which cannot override the media rule hiding the panel.

## Why and why now

Pin currently makes an otherwise reachable jelly disappear for phone users. Both rules causing this remain at the base. No dedicated direction document exists; the README describes usable jellies that people can react to, and losing access defeats that purpose. Issue #44 and open PR #49 address stream-title spacing, not access to pinned jellies; closed PR #42 addresses full-view headers. Tracker searches found no covering issue or pull request.

## Outcome

Phone users can visibly discover, reopen and unpin every pinned jelly, including jellies with no pending attention, immediately after pinning and after reload.

## Invariants

Preserve pin persistence, project filtering, desktop pinned access and existing full-view controls. Use existing state-changing operations. Keep stored and customized jelly pages under the documented explicit reshape procedure.

## Acceptance

- At 390px, pin a stream jelly with no pending attention; a visible touch-accessible route still lets the person reopen and unpin it.
- Reload and verify that the same route remains available.
- Verify multiple pinned jellies and the 900px/901px layout boundary without unreachable controls or horizontal overflow.
- Confirm desktop pinned access and project filtering remain usable.
- Verify using the bundled view in an isolated home; run `cargo test` and `cargo clippy`.
